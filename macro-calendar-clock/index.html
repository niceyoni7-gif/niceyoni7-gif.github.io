Macro Calendar Clock

Real-time macro event countdown for day traders on TradingView.

What this extension does

  • Displays a live countdown overlay on TradingView charts to the next upcoming high/medium-impact economic event (e.g. CPI, NFP, Fed rate decisions).
  • Lets you open a full economic calendar panel directly on the chart, powered by a public Forex Factory data feed.
  • Customizable filters: impact level (high only / medium+ / all), date range (today / today+tomorrow / this week), country/currency, and visual skin.
  • Runs entirely in the browser — lightweight, no background processing beyond periodic calendar refreshes.

Who it's for

Day traders and technical analysts who want to stay aware of upcoming macro-economic events without leaving their TradingView chart to check an external calendar.

Privacy

This extension stores your preferences (impact level, selected countries, date range, display skin) locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. These settings never leave your browser.

To display economic events, the extension fetches public calendar data from a third-party feed (Forex Factory). No personal data, browsing history, trading account information, or TradingView account details are collected, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

The extension does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services of any kind.
