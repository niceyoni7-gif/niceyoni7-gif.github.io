Macro Calender Clock

A Chrome extension designed to help you track time, manage calendar events, and optimize your daily workflow directly from your browser.

What this app does

  • Display calendar metrics, time tracking, and custom clock macro tools.
  • Quick desktop access to scheduled events and tasks.
  • Lightweight performance without heavily impacting system resources.
  • Store settings and cached data locally on your device or securely via integration.

Who it is for

Users who need an efficient way to keep their calendar schedules visible and managed without leaving their active tabs.

Privacy

We respect your privacy. Data transmission is kept strictly to essential service syncs, and your configuration data remains private.
