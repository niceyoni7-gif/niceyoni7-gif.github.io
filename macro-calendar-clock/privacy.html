Privacy Policy for Macro Calendar Clock

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

Macro Calendar Clock ("the Extension") is designed with absolute privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, financial data, or TradingView account details.

2. Local Storage

The Extension uses Chrome Extension Storage (locally on your device) solely to save your specific user preferences, such as:

  • Selected countries and currencies
  • Event importance filters
  • Date range selections
  • Display themes (light/dark mode)

This data never leaves your browser.

3. Third-Party Services

To display the economic events, the Extension fetches publicly available macroeconomic calendar data from Forex Factory. This is a one-way data retrieval process. No information about you, your machine, or your trading activity is sent to Forex Factory or any other third-party server.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The Extension does not contain any analytics software, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks.
