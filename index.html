NiceYoni Multi-Account Mail Notifier

A Chrome extension that helps you monitor and manage unread Gmail inbox messages from multiple Google accounts in one place — with desktop alerts, quick actions, and per-account settings.

What this app does

  • Connect several Gmail accounts via secure Google OAuth sign-in.
  • Show unread Inbox messages in a single popup.
  • Send desktop notifications when new unread mail arrives.
  • Let you mark read, archive, star, spam, or delete — only when you choose.
  • Store settings and cached mail data locally on your device only.

Who it is for

People who use more than one Gmail account and want a lightweight notifier without switching browser profiles.
